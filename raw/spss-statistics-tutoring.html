What a session actually covers

A tutoring session is not a lecture and it is not us doing the analysis while you watch. It is structured, hands-on guidance built around four moves that repeat across the whole chapter.

1. Test selection

We start from your research questions and hypotheses, your variable types (nominal, ordinal, scale) and your sample, then reason together toward the right test. This is the step markers probe hardest, so we make sure you can justify the choice yourself.

2. Running it

Screen-sharing your own machine, you click through the analysis while your tutor explains each dialog, option and assumption check (normality, homogeneity, multicollinearity). You end the session able to repeat it without us.

3. Reading the output

SPSS produces far more than you report. We show you which tables and figures matter, what the significance values, effect sizes and confidence intervals mean, and what to ignore. For a deeper walk-through, see our sibling guide on SPSS output explained.

4. Writing it up

Finally we coach the narrative: how to report each result in your referencing style, how to phrase findings without overclaiming, and how to link results back to your literature. You write every sentence; we give feedback until it reads like sound academic work.
